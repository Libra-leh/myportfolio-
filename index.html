Non-Academic · Fine Arts

Painting & Fine Arts

I've been painting since 2020, self-taught in oil and acrylic. During Covid-19, I had nothing to do other than gaming and doomscrolling. That's when I know I have to take up a leisure activity. I spent my allowance on art supplies, slowly experiment my way while watching real-time painting videos in Youtube. Art is how I slow down, observe the world, and process ideas that nothing can't quite express.

My subjects tend to be landscapes. I paint mostly on weekends or whenever I feel boring.

Whenever I run out of idea, I prefer to walk alone in darkness, observing the interplay of light and shadow.This might not give me an idea immediately, but gradually
